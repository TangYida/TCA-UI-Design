Introduction

The China Academy is an intellectual content network dedicated to helping global audiences understand the key dynamics that are driving how China sees the world.

The China Academy team has built one of China’s most influential current affairs and intellectual content outlet, with over 150 million subscribers across Chinese social media. This, combined with extensive connections with China’s top intellectuals, policy advisors and business stakeholders, enables our team to deliver the most trusted analysis of what's unfolding in China and on-the-ground sentiments.

The China Academy is a gateway to understanding the fundamental logics of how China operates. We provide video programs and digital courses on www.thechinaacademy.org, as well as through a network of distribution channels including mainstream broadcasters and ditigal media worldwide. We also run events and conferences, and have a long history of offering research and advisory work servicing government and corporate institutions.

What we offer

Media

Sourcing original content and critical insights from China and around the world, with the aim of encouraging communication in search of new global narratives

Education

Original courses and exclusive interviews in collaboration with scholars, experts and leading influencers, helping stakeholders from around the world raise their China IQ

Events

Virtual and in-person forums on China and the world, bringing together scholars, practitioners, and decision-makers for open dialogue.

Travel

Research and Advisory

A boutique research team made up of international professionals, offering tailor-made research and education programs. Our team is embedded in the China media and academic ecosystem, with influential connections, partners and affiliates across business, politics and society. Starting 2022, we have expanded services to the boardrooms and C-suites of companies and investors with significant exposure to China.
