Certified Courses

HSK is the most popular and widely applied Chinese language proficiency test.

HSK assesses Chinese learners' abilities to use Chinese as a second language in their daily, academic, and professional lives through a standardized examination and evaluation with a uniform grading and scoring system.

HSK is the world' s third-largest language test brand after IELTS and TOEFL. HSK scores have become a requirement to prove Chinese language proficiency for international students to apply for admission, graduation, and scholarship in China. For foreigners looking to work in China, a high HSK score can help better the chances of obtaining a working visa.

HSK certificates are widely applied in China in the following scenarios：

  • College enrollment, levelled learning, course exemption and course credits
  • Employee recruitment, training and advancement
  • Evaluation of the quality of teaching at educational institutions
  • Credible and comprehensive structure to assist self-learning Chinese

Requirements

  • Self-study online for at least 36 hours (HSK1&2) or 30 hours (YCT1&2).
  • Produce the spoken and written texts in each section.
  • Complete the mid-course test.
  • Pass at least 1 HSK Mock test for your level.

Certificate

  • When you have satisfied the requirements above, select the test results you would like to include and submit your records for review.
  • Receive your official HSK or YCT certificate by email.

Benefits

  • HSK or YCT certificate of Chinese language proficiency that is internationally recognised, without you having to go to a test center to sit an exam.
  • Innovative methodology, developed specifically for non-native speakers learning Chinese, makes the language as simple for you to acquire as possible.
  • Designed by both native and non-native teachers of Chinese with decades of international experience.
  • Produced in partnership with Chinese Testing International, the responsible for the HSK and YCT qualifications globally.

What you learn

  • All the prescribed vocabulary for HSK or YCT Levels 1 and 2 tests.
  • How to understand and pronounce that vocabulary in spoken form.
  • How to read and write that vocabulary in Chinese characters.
  • The Pinyin system of writing out the sounds of Chinese phonetically, so that you can type characters on a phone or computer.
  • How to produce sentences in both spoken and written form with confidence.

Start communicating in Chinese and lay down a solid foundation for future language studies.

How it works

There are two Pathways through the course so that you can

  • Focus separately on your spoken and written Chinese.

The pathways are divided into Sections, each with a theme and objectives.

  • The goal of each Section is for you to say or write a “text” in Chinese.

Build up to each “text” one simple step at a time by:

  • Picking up a new character
  • Using it to unlock multi-character words
  • Putting those words into sentences
  • There are also explanations of sentence patterns to help you.

Along the way

  • You must complete the starred items on each Pathway. The others are bonus material.
  • Halfway through there is a compulsory mid-course test, so that you can see how you are doing. There is also an optional test at the end of the course if you’d like it.
  • In each section, there are additional auto-generated and teacher-made exercises if you wish to do extra practice –flashcards, pairs matching, true/false, printable character-tracing sheets etc.
  • You have up to a year to get to the summit, and pass at least one test at HSK Mock.

At the top

  • When you reach the summit, you have covered all the material you may be tested on.
  • Hop over to hskmock.com (or the HSK Mock mobile app) and take an official HSK or YCT practice test on your computer or phone.
  • When you have passed at least one test, go back to your Certified Course dashboard to select the test results you are most proud of and submit your records for review.
  • Assuming everything is in order, your HSK or YCT certificate from Chinese Testing International will be emailed to you within a few days.
