Chang’e-6 Returns to Earth with World’s First Lunar Far Side Sample

In this briefing

At 2:07 PM Beijing time, the Chang’e-6 return capsule successfully landed in the predetermined area of Siziwang Banner, Inner Mongolia, marking the complete success of the mission and the world’s first sample return from the far side of the Moon.

The mission launched on May 3, landed on the lunar far side on June 1, and returned approximately two kilograms of rocks, soil and regolith for storage, analysis and research.

OpenAI to Discontinue API Services in China

OpenAI announced that it would cease API services to unsupported countries, including China, beginning July 9. Domestic large-language-model providers subsequently introduced transition plans for affected developers.

Japanese Citizens Attacked in Suzhou; Chinese Citizen Seriously Injured While Intervening

A knife attack in Suzhou injured two Japanese citizens and left a Chinese citizen who intervened in critical condition. Police apprehended the suspect and described the incident as an isolated case under investigation.
