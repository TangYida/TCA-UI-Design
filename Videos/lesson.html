Instructor

Yin Zhiguang

Professor of International Politics at Fudan University, with research on China’s place in the Global South and the politics of de-dependency.

This seminar examines how colonialism and later neoliberal globalization locked much of the Global South into growth without development. Yin Zhiguang asks whether China belongs to the Global South community and what the Bandung Spirit offers contemporary struggles for productive and political autonomy.
