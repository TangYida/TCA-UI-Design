About this lecture

This seminar is presented by Prof. Yin Zhiguang of Fudan University, who is known for his insights into China’s role in the Global South.

Prof. Yin reviews how colonialism, followed by neoliberal globalization, shaped the Global South, resulting in growth without development and structural dependence on Western countries.

He also addresses the sensitive question of whether China belongs to the Global South community, and examines the continuing relevance of the spirit of the Bandung Conference for countries in the Global South.

Questions to carry through the lesson

  • How is political independence limited by economic and institutional dependence?
  • What did Bandung offer beyond a single diplomatic meeting?
  • Where does China sit within the historical and political idea of the Global South?
